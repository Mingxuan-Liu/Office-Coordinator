Grid & replicate

Replicate copies the selected region along a row or column. This is the fastest way to lay out a bank of identical desks.

Select exactly one region to enable replication.

Assist: suggest rectangles

Heuristic suggestion only — never the source of truth. It grayscales the loaded bitmap, thresholds it, and finds light regions fully enclosed by dark lines. On a clean line-drawing plan that finds desk outlines; it will also miss desks and invent false ones wherever a line has a gap or a label crosses a border. Nothing here enters the export until you accept it.
